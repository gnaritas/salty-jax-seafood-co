Our Story

A lobster roll
looking for a Coast

Restaurants and seafood shacks selling lobster rolls are common on the East Coast. In South Mississippi, they were almost unknown — so Ngoc Nguyen and Lu Duong came to fix that themselves.

2022

The truck

Salty Jax hit the road as a food truck, bringing New England-style lobster rolls — with Gulf shrimp and Louisiana crawfish — to parking lots and festivals across the Mississippi Gulf Coast.

The truck ran on one promise: the rolls you cross the North for, made with Gulf catch, posted daily on social so fans could follow it town to town.

2024 · 2025

Best Food Truck on the Coast — twice

Voted Best Food Truck in 2024 and 2025 in WXXV’s VCA’s, and Best of Coastal Mississippi 2025 by Sun Herald readers. The Scout Guide Mississippi Gulf Coast featured us, and 23,000+ fans followed the truck.

Feb 2026

The experiment: Crave Food Hall

We moved into a counter inside Crave Food Hall at OS 1515 on Government Street in downtown Ocean Springs — to learn what a sit-down kitchen would feel like. Many days we sold out of bisque, chowder and every last roll. Our last day at Crave was Sunday, May 31.

2026

Home: The Shoppes at Washington Avenue

Now we’ve opened our own four walls at The Shoppes at Washington Avenue, off I-10 Exit 50. Soft-launching with a limited menu — and the food truck keeps rolling across Gautier, Diamondhead, Pascagoula and the rest of the Coast, posting its stop every day on social.

What’s next

“The ultimate goal is to grow into a fast-casual chain in Southern beach towns like Destin, Florida.”

— Lu Duong, co-owner, in the Biloxi Sun Herald

Ocean Springs is address number one. The roll stays the same: North Atlantic technique, Gulf catch, Cajun soul.
