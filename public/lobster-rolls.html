The Signature

Four rolls. Two coasts.
One bun.

Lobster rolls are rare on the Mississippi Gulf Coast. We came from the North to fix that — then added a roll you won’t find in New England at all.

Full Menu Get Directions

1Connecticut — warm, buttered

Fresh lobster piled into a toasted, buttered top-split bun, then warmed with drawn butter. No mayo, no lettuce, no argument. This is how Connecticut does it, and our regulars order it that way.

2Maine — chilled, with mayo

Lobster tossed with mayonnaise and served cold, exactly as the northern New England shore serves it. Cool, creamy, and the right call on a 90-degree Coast afternoon.

3Maryland — lump crab

For the crab camp: lump crabmeat in the same toasted bun, Chesapeake style.

4Bayou — crawfish, Cajun garlic butter

Our Coast’s own answer: warm Louisiana crawfish, tossed in Cajun garlic butter. Red shrimp and crawfish give every order a Southern twist the North can’t copy.

Not a lobster person? No problem.

Substitute shrimp for lobster in any roll — Cajun, Connecticut or Maine style. Same bun, same technique, your catch.
